I design to close the gap between intent and action.

I'm an award winning Senior Product Designer based in Dubai, with seven years scaling products across travel, fintech, and enterprise platforms. The moments I care about most are where users hesitate, abandon, or lose trust, most of my strongest work has happened right there.

I've spent that time across two very different environments. At Sastaticket, a travel platform serving 500,000 monthly users, I grew from intern to Lead Product Designer, owning core booking and conversion flows end to end. At MRM McCann, I've led design across enterprise platforms including NEOM, Kuwait Finance House, Gucci, McDonald's, and Dubai Airports, and several others spanning fintech, luxury retail, hospitality, and aviation.

How I Think

Principles I follow dearly

I understand the problem before jumping into design.

Moving the metric matters. So does the craft.

Tradeoffs are part of the job. Not everything can ship and that's fine.

When engineering has hard limits, I solve what design can fix on its own first.

I believe that delightful experiences foster trust.

Outcomes over outputs. Delivering is not the same as solving.

I collaborate with product and engineering. Design doesn't happen in isolation.

Feedback is on the design, not on me. Design is iterative, never perfect.

Engineering constraints are real. I design with them considered.
