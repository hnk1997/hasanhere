About me

I design for the moment someone hesitates before completing a booking or a payment.

Seven years in product design, most of it spent on high traffic consumer platforms where conversion and trust decide whether someone follows through. I've redesigned core booking flows that cut drop-off by 19%, validated through A/B testing and held across every season, not a one-time spike. I've shipped features focused specifically on payment trust and reducing transaction anxiety at checkout, work that's given me a real interest in fintech and the psychology of money moments.

That same thinking now carries into enterprise design work, digital experiences & bilingual design systems built for institutions operating at serious scale and stakes. That work has been recognised at Cannes Lions, the industry's most prestigious award, for a digital experience I helped shape from the ground up.

Principles I follow dearly

I understand the problem before jumping into design.

Moving the metric matters. So does the craft.

Tradeoffs are part of the job. Not everything can ship and that's fine.

When engineering has hard limits, I solve what design can fix on its own first.

I believe that delightful experiences foster trust.

Outcomes over outputs. Delivering is not the same as solving.

I collaborate with product and engineering. Design doesn't happen in isolation.

Feedback is on the design, not on me. Design is iterative, never perfect.

Engineering constraints are real. I design with them considered.
